# Python Practice: how we use your information

Python Practice is a website that helps you practise programming in Python. This notice explains what it stores
about you, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

## What is stored

- **Your nickname**, which you choose when you join (not your real name), and **which class** you are in.
- **Your login code.** It is stored encrypted, so it can't be read from the database. Only your teacher can see it, if you
  forget it, and every time they look it is recorded.
- **Your PIN.** It is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that can't be turned back into your PIN. No one can see
  it, not even your teacher.
- **Your progress and attempts:** your level in each unit, which tasks you passed or didn't, the hints you used, and
  why an answer didn't pass (for example a spelling mistake in a variable name).
- **The program code and output from attempts that didn't pass, for 30 days.** After 30 days they are deleted. What
  kind of mistake it was is kept, so your teacher can still see what to help with.

Python Practice does not ask for your real name, email address, date of birth or photo, and it does not use tracking or
advertising.

## Why

So that you can carry on from any computer, so the practice can pick tasks at the right level for you, and so your
teacher can see where you, and the class, need help.

## How long it is kept

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Program code and output from attempts that didn't pass | 30 days |
| Nickname, login code, progress and attempts | Until the end of the school year (31 August), or sooner if your teacher deletes your class or you delete your progress |
| PIN (hashed) | The same as above |

At the end of the school year your class and everything in it is deleted automatically.

## Who processes it

The information is stored by **Supabase**, an online database company, on servers in the **European Union (Ireland)**.
Supabase stores it for [SCHOOL NAME] and doesn't use it for anything else.

In the website, only your teacher can see your class's information. The people who run the service for
[SCHOOL NAME] can also reach the database, to keep it working.

## Deleting it

- **You** can delete everything saved for you at any time: on the Python Practice home page, choose
  **Delete everything saved for me**. It is deleted straight away. To practise again you join your class again.
- **Your teacher** can delete you from the class, or delete the whole class.
- You can also ask [CONTACT] to delete it for you.
