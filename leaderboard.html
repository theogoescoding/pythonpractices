Each unit gives up to 300 points. Secured points: up to 200. You get 2 points for each percent of a secured unit's score, so 100% gives 200 and 50% gives 100. Level points: up to 100. You get 10 points for each level, so level 10 gives 100. A unit you haven't started, or still have the quick check to do in, gives 0 level points.

Two more totals come on top of that. Programme points: each level 10 writing task you pass in endless mode gives 50 points the first time. Each hint takes off 10, and "Show me how" counts as a hint. You always get at least 10. Passing it again gives nothing. Re-secure bonus: each time a unit drops and you bring it back to 100%, you get a star and 10 points. A unit can have up to 5 stars, so up to 50 points. Your points are all four added together.
